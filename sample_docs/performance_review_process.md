# Acme Technologies - Performance Management & Review Process

## 1. Philosophy & Purpose
At Acme Technologies, performance management is designed to cultivate continuous growth, recognize merit, align individual goals with organizational outcomes, and reward high achievers fairly and objectively.

## 2. Review Cycles & Timelines
Acme Technologies follows a **bi-annual performance review rhythm**:
- **Mid-Year Check-in (H1):** Conducted in July/August. Focuses on developmental feedback, course correction, and goal alignment without direct compensation revisions.
- **Annual Performance Review (Annual Cycle):** Conducted in January/February, assessing full fiscal year impact. Determines annual merit increments, promotions, and performance bonuses.

## 3. The 4-Stage Performance Evaluation Process

### Stage 1: Goal Setting (OKRs)
At the start of each cycle, employees collaborate with managers to formulate 3 to 5 Objectives and Key Results (OKRs). OKRs must follow SMART criteria (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).

### Stage 2: Self-Appraisal
Employees reflect on their accomplishments, project milestones, challenges overcome, and skill advancements over the evaluation period in the HRMS Performance module.

### Stage 3: Peer & 360-Degree Feedback
Employees select 2 to 3 cross-functional collaborators to provide constructive peer feedback. Peers evaluate collaboration, technical agility, leadership, and communication.

### Stage 4: Manager Assessment & Calibration
Reporting managers review self-assessments and peer feedback, provide narrative evaluations, and assign rating scores. Department heads participate in normalization calibration sessions to ensure rating parity across departments.

## 4. Rating Scale (5-Point Framework)
- **5.0 - Far Exceeds Expectations:** Exceptional leadership and transformative impact significantly beyond role scope. (Reserved for top 5-10% of team).
- **4.0 - Exceeds Expectations:** Consistently surpasses deliverables with superior quality, initiative, and proactive teamwork.
- **3.0 - Meets Expectations:** Consistently satisfies all core job responsibilities, goals, and project deadlines with dependable quality.
- **2.0 - Needs Improvement:** Partially achieves objectives; demonstrates gaps in technical execution, communication, or timely delivery.
- **1.0 - Unsatisfactory:** Consistently falls short of essential expectations; immediate remediation required.

## 5. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees receiving a rating below 2.5 or exhibiting persistent underperformance are placed on a structured 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- The PIP details clear milestones, weekly mentor check-ins, and tangible success metrics.
- Failure to meet defined outcomes by day 60 may lead to role reassignment or termination of employment.

## 6. Promotions and Compensation Impact
Merit increments and grade promotions are finalized following the Annual Review cycle. Increment letters are released in March, with adjusted payouts effective April 1.
