# Acme Technologies - Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Objective and Applicability
This policy provides guidelines for the timely, equitable, and transparent reimbursement of legitimate business, travel, and productivity-related expenses incurred by employees on behalf of Acme Technologies.

## 2. Eligible Expense Categories

### 2.1 Broadband & Mobile Connectivity Allowance
- Full-time regular employees working under hybrid or remote work arrangements are entitled to a monthly internet and telephony reimbursement of up to **INR 1,500** (or $20 USD).
- Employees must submit a valid digital monthly invoice and proof of payment through the HRMS expense module.

### 2.2 Learning & Professional Development Allowance
- Every full-time employee is eligible for an annual professional learning stipend of **INR 25,000** (approx $300 USD) per fiscal year.
- Qualifying expenses include technical certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP, CKA), relevant Coursera/Udemy/O'Reilly subscriptions, and industry conferences.
- Prior approval from the reporting manager is mandatory before purchasing course vouchers.

### 2.3 Official Business Travel & Commute
- **Local Business Commute:** Actual cab fare or mileage for client visits within the city (Uber/Ola corporate rides or standard rates) is fully reimbursable upon attaching trip receipts.
- **Domestic Flights:** Economy class bookings on designated corporate travel portals (MakeMyTrip Corporate) are required for inter-city travel.
- **Daily Per Diem (Meals & Incidentals):** Employees traveling out-of-station on authorized company assignments are entitled to a per diem meal allowance of **INR 1,200 per day** for tier-1 cities (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR) and **INR 800 per day** for tier-2 cities.
- **Hotel Stays:** Corporate tie-up hotels up to INR 4,500 per night for regular staff and INR 6,500 for managers.

### 2.4 Team Outings & Celebrations
- Managers are allocated a quarterly team engagement budget of **INR 1,500 per team member** for offsites, team dinners, and sprint celebrations.

## 3. Submission Deadlines & Documentation
1. **Invoice Requirement:** Original itemized tax invoices with GSTIN numbers (where applicable) must be attached. Credit card charge slips alone are not sufficient.
2. **Timeline:** Expense claims must be submitted within **30 days** of incurring the expense. Late submissions past 60 days will be rejected unless an exceptional waiver is granted by Finance.
3. **Approval Cycle:** Reporting managers review and approve claims within 5 business days, followed by Finance audit. Approved funds are disbursed in the next monthly payroll cycle.
