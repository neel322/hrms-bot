# Acme Technologies - Attendance and Work From Home (WFH) Policy

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
Acme Technologies fosters a culture of ownership, trust, and accountability. This policy establishes operating guidelines for regular working hours, hybrid working models, attendance logging, and remote work protocols.

## 2. Standard Working Hours & Schedule
- **Standard Working Week:** Monday through Friday (5-day work week). Saturday and Sunday are official weekly offs.
- **Core Business Hours:** 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM (Local Office Time), including a mandatory 1-hour meal and rest break.
- **Minimum Daily Expectation:** Full-time staff are expected to log at least 8 effective working hours per day (40 hours per calendar week).
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** All team members are expected to be available for meetings and core communications between 10:30 AM and 4:30 PM.

## 3. Attendance Tracking & Clock-in Protocols
1. **Attendance System:** Daily check-in and check-out must be recorded through the HRMS attendance portal or biometric access card readers at Acme office hubs.
2. **Grace Period:** A morning grace window of 15 minutes (until 9:45 AM) is permitted for unexpected transit delays.
3. **Late Arrivals:** More than 3 late check-ins in a calendar month without prior managerial dispensation will result in the deduction of a half-day Casual Leave.
4. **Regularization:** If an employee forgets to clock in or out due to technical glitches, an Attendance Regularization Request must be submitted in the HRMS within 48 hours, subject to manager sign-off.

## 4. Hybrid and Remote Work (WFH) Guidelines

### 4.1 Hybrid Office Model
- Employees in engineering, support, and corporate operations operate on a hybrid 3+2 schedule:
  - **In-Office Days:** Minimum 3 designated days per week at their local tech hub (e.g., Bangalore, Pune, Mumbai).
  - **Remote Days:** Up to 2 days per week from home or an approved remote location.
- Department heads and team leads establish designated in-office collaboration days (e.g., Tuesday and Thursday) for team syncs.

### 4.2 Full-Time Remote Work Exceptions
- Full-time remote arrangements require formal sign-off from the Department Head and the HR Director (Anita Desai).
- Employees operating remotely must maintain a dependable high-speed broadband connection (minimum 50 Mbps) and a dedicated, ergonomic workspace.

### 4.3 WFH Communication Standards
- Team members working remotely must remain responsive on Slack and email during core collaboration hours.
- Video camera presence is encouraged for client interactions and team design reviews.
- Any midday unreachability exceeding 30 minutes must be communicated on team channels.

## 5. Overtime and Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
Non-exempt engineering and support personnel required to support scheduled weekend production deployments or urgent client emergency escalations are entitled to 1 compensatory off day per weekend day worked. Comp-off must be availed within 60 days of accrual upon manager authorization.
