# Acme Technologies - Leave & Time-Off Policy

## 1. Overview & Objectives
Acme Technologies recognizes the importance of work-life balance and personal well-being. This policy outlines guidelines for leave entitlements, eligibility, request processes, and approval workflows for all full-time regular employees.

## 2. Leave Categories and Annual Entitlements

The leave calendar at Acme Technologies runs from **January 1 to December 31**. Entitlements are credited on the first calendar day of the year or pro-rated based on the joining date for new hires.

### 2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
- **Entitlement:** 12 days per calendar year.
- **Purpose:** Short-term personal obligations, urgent family events, domestic emergencies, or personal downtime.
- **Booking Rules:** Casual leaves can be taken up to a maximum of 3 consecutive business days. Requires at least 24 hours prior submission in the HRMS, except in unforeseen emergencies.
- **Encashment & Carry Forward:** Casual leaves cannot be encashed and do not carry forward into the next calendar year; unused CL lapses on December 31.

### 2.2 Sick / Medical Leave (SL)
- **Entitlement:** 10 days per calendar year.
- **Purpose:** Personal illness, hospitalization, recuperation, or scheduled medical treatments.
- **Certification Requirement:** For sick leave extending beyond 2 consecutive business days, a verified medical certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner must be uploaded to the HRMS portal upon resumption of duties.
- **Carry Forward:** Unused sick leaves do not carry forward and cannot be encashed.

### 2.3 Earned / Privilege Leave (EL)
- **Entitlement:** 18 days per calendar year (accrued at 1.5 days per completed calendar month).
- **Purpose:** Extended vacations, annual holidays, rest, and leisure.
- **Booking Rules:** Must be applied at least 10 business days in advance in the HRMS to allow adequate resource planning. A minimum of 3 consecutive business days must be requested per instance.
- **Carry Forward:** Up to 15 unused earned leave days can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. The maximum cumulative cap is 45 days. Any excess beyond 45 days is forfeited.
- **Encashment:** Earned leave can be encashed solely at the time of separation or retirement based on basic salary.

### 2.4 Maternity & Paternity Leave
- **Maternity Leave:** Female employees who have completed at least 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months are entitled to 26 weeks (180 calendar days) of fully paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children. Additional leave up to 4 weeks may be granted upon medical certification for complications.
- **Paternity Leave:** Male employees are entitled to 15 calendar days of fully paid paternity leave, which may be availed within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.

### 2.5 Bereavement Leave
- All employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive business days of paid leave in the unfortunate event of the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law).

## 3. Leave Application and Approval Workflow
1. **Submission:** All requests must be submitted through the Acme HRMS portal (`/leave-requests`).
2. **Approval Hierarchy:** Requests are routed directly to the employee's designated reporting manager.
3. **Turnaround Time:** Reporting managers are required to approve or reject leave requests within 48 hours of submission.
4. **Emergency Leave:** In case of emergency sickness, the employee or their representative must notify the manager via Slack or email by 10:00 AM on the day of absence and file the HRMS record within 48 hours.

## 4. Unplanned Absences & Disciplinary Actions
Unexcused absence without prior approval or notification exceeding 3 consecutive business days will be deemed as job abandonment and will trigger HR inquiry.
