# Acme Technologies - Employee Benefits and Health Insurance Policy

## 1. Overview
Acme Technologies is dedicated to the total health, well-being, and financial security of our employees and their families. This document outlines our comprehensive benefits package, including group health insurance, wellness programs, and statutory safeguards.

## 2. Group Medical Insurance (GMC)
- **Sum Insured:** Acme provides a baseline Group Medical Cover of **INR 5,00,000** (approx $6,000 USD) per family per policy year, 100% premium sponsored by the company.
- **Coverage Scope (1+3 / 1+5):** Covers employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children. Dependent parents/in-laws can be added under a voluntary parental top-up scheme at negotiated corporate group premium rates.
- **Pre-Existing Diseases:** Covered from Day 1 with zero waiting period.
- **Room Rent Capping:** 1% of Sum Insured per day for regular rooms; 2% per day for ICU admissions.
- **Maternity Coverage:** Normal delivery covered up to INR 50,000; Caesarean section covered up to INR 75,000. New-born baby is covered from Day 1 within the overall family floater.
- **Cashless Hospitalization:** Available across 8,500+ network hospitals via the insurance TPA mobile app (MediBuddy / Vidal Health). Pre-authorization must be initiated 48 hours prior for planned admissions, or within 24 hours for emergencies.

## 3. Group Personal Accident (GPA) & Term Life Insurance (GTL)
- **Group Term Life Insurance:** Provides coverage equivalent to **3x Annual Fixed Cost to Company (CTC)**, payable to designated nominees in the unfortunate event of death.
- **Group Personal Accident:** Provides financial protection up to **2x Annual Fixed CTC** in cases of permanent total disability, permanent partial disability, or accidental demise.

## 4. Wellness and Mental Health Support
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Confidential 24/7 telephonic and video counseling sessions with certified psychologists and wellness therapists for employees and immediate family members.
- **Annual Health Checkup:** Fully covered annual executive health checkups at partner diagnostic centers (Apollo / Lal PathLabs) for employees aged 30 and above.
- **Gym & Fitness Reimbursement:** Corporate wellness discount partnerships with Cult.fit and local fitness centers, plus an optional gym allowance of INR 1,000/month under flexible benefits.

## 5. Retirement and Statutory Benefits
- **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF):** Governed under the EPFO statutory guidelines. 12% employee contribution matched equally by employer.
- **Gratuity:** Payable in accordance with the Payment of Gratuity Act upon completion of 5 continuous years of service at Acme Technologies.
- **National Pension System (NPS):** Optional corporate NPS scheme available under Section 80CCD(2) for additional tax-advantaged retirement savings.

## 6. How to File Insurance Claims
1. Log in to the HRMS Benefits portal or TPA mobile application.
2. For reimbursement claims (non-network hospitals): Submit original discharge summary, hospital bills, pharmacy receipts, and doctor prescriptions within 30 days of hospital discharge.
3. For escalation assistance, contact Kavita Rao (`benefits@acmetech.com`).
