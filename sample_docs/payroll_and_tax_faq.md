# Acme Technologies - Payroll, Compensation & Tax FAQs

## 1. Payroll Calendar & Salary Disbursement
- **Disbursement Date:** Salaries are disbursed directly to registered bank accounts on the **last working day of each calendar month**. If the last day is a weekend or banking holiday, payroll is processed on the preceding working day.
- **Payslip Availability:** Digital payslips are generated and downloadable via the Acme HRMS portal (`/payroll`) within 24 hours of salary credit.

## 2. Salary Structure & Components
A typical compensation package at Acme Technologies includes the following key salary components:
- **Basic Salary:** Constitutes approximately 50% of Total Fixed Pay. Serves as the benchmark for statutory benefits, Provident Fund (PF), and Gratuity.
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** Calculated at 40% to 50% of Basic Salary depending on metro or non-metro tax jurisdiction.
- **Special Allowance / Flexible Benefits:** Flexible basket covering transport, books and periodicals, meal cards, and uniform maintenance.
- **Provident Fund (PF):** Statutory deduction of 12% of Basic Salary matched with an equal employer contribution.

## 3. Income Tax & TDS Deductions
### 3.1 Tax Regimes
Employees can choose between the **New Tax Regime** (default, lower slab rates without exemptions) and the **Old Tax Regime** (allows Section 80C, 80D, HRA, and home loan interest deductions). The tax regime selection must be made at the beginning of each fiscal year (April) in the HRMS.

### 3.2 Tax Declaration & Proof Submission
- **Provisional Declaration:** Submitted in April/May to determine monthly Tax Deducted at Source (TDS).
- **Final Proof Submission Window:** Opens on December 15 and closes on January 20. Actual receipts for ELSS, life insurance, medical insurance, rent agreements, and tuition fees must be uploaded.
- Failure to submit proofs will result in recalculation of TDS based on standard slabs in February and March payslips.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: How can I change my salary bank account?**  
A: Navigate to HRMS > Profile > Financial Details and upload a cancelled cheque or bank statement showing your name, account number, and IFSC code. Changes submitted before the 15th take effect in the current month's payroll.

**Q2: What is Form 16, and when will it be issued?**  
A: Form 16 is the annual certificate of tax deducted at source issued by Acme Technologies. It is released on the HRMS portal on or before June 15 following the close of the financial year.

**Q3: Are variable pay and performance bonuses subject to TDS?**  
A: Yes, all bonuses and performance incentives are taxable and subject to TDS according to your prevailing income tax slab in the month of payout.

**Q4: Whom should I contact for discrepancies in my payslip?**  
A: You can raise a payroll ticket in HRMS or email `payroll@acmetech.com`. The Finance team (Karthik Pillai / Suresh Singhania) resolves tickets within 3 business days.
