# Acme Technologies - New Hire Onboarding Guide

## 1. Welcome to Acme Technologies!
Welcome to the team! This guide will help you navigate your first 90 days at Acme Technologies smoothly, understand key workplace tools, and complete your mandatory setup tasks.

## 2. Pre-Boarding & Day 1 Checklist

### 2.1 Day 1 Schedule
- **09:30 AM - Welcome Session:** Orientation kickoff with Anita Desai and Kavita Rao from People Operations.
- **11:00 AM - IT Asset Provisioning:** Laptop handover (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad T14), monitor assignment, and Single Sign-On (SSO) credential activation.
- **02:00 PM - Buddy Introduction:** Meet your assigned onboarding buddy over lunch or virtual coffee.
- **03:30 PM - Team Introduction:** Join your functional department sync (Engineering, Sales, etc.) hosted by your reporting manager.

### 2.2 Mandatory Documentation Submission
Within your first 3 days, complete the document verification checklist on the HRMS portal:
- Government Identity Proof (Aadhaar / PAN Card / Passport)
- Highest educational degree certificates and transcripts
- Relieving letter and experience certificates from previous employers
- Signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Code of Conduct acknowledgment
- Bank account details and cancelled cheque for payroll deposit

## 3. Workplace Tools & Essential Accounts
Your Single Sign-On (Google Workspace / Okta) provides instant access to the core Acme toolchain:
- **Slack:** Primary internal collaboration tool. Join `#general`, `#announcements`, `#tech-chat`, and your specific team channels.
- **Jira & Confluence:** Sprint planning, task tracking, and technical engineering documentation.
- **GitHub Enterprise:** Code repositories and pull request reviews.
- **Acme HRMS (`hrms.acmetech.internal`):** Leave tracking, attendance clock-in, payroll payslips, and performance appraisals.

## 4. 30-60-90 Day Milestones

### First 30 Days: Assimilation & Setup
- Complete mandatory compliance trainings (POSH, Data Privacy, InfoSec).
- Set up local development environment or department tooling.
- Weekly 1-on-1 touchpoints with your reporting manager to define key expectations.

### Day 31 to 60: Active Contribution
- Take ownership of your first standalone feature story or business project.
- Engage in code reviews, design documentation, and peer pairing.
- Mid-probation review check-in with your manager.

### Day 61 to 90: Independence & Probation Confirmation
- Independent execution of core responsibilities.
- Complete probation review in HRMS. Upon managerial recommendation and HR sign-off, official employment confirmation status is granted.
