# Acme Technologies - Employee Code of Conduct & Ethics

## 1. Introduction & Ethical Core
Acme Technologies is committed to uncompromising standards of integrity, transparency, mutual respect, and legal compliance. Every employee represents Acme Technologies in their words, decisions, and digital interactions.

## 2. Workplace Respect & Anti-Harassment
- **Zero Tolerance:** Acme Technologies strictly prohibits any form of discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, age, caste, or marital status.
- **Inclusive Culture:** Every colleague must be treated with professional courtesy and dignity. Demeaning remarks, bullying, or microaggressions are grounds for disciplinary action.
- For issues involving sexual harassment, refer to the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy.

## 3. Conflict of Interest & Outside Engagements
- **Dual Employment:** Full-time Acme employees are strictly prohibited from engaging in commercial dual employment ("moonlighting") or providing paid advisory services to competing software entities.
- **Personal Investments:** Direct financial investments in active vendors, direct competitors, or clients must be disclosed to HR and Legal.
- **Gifts and Hospitality:** Employees may not accept gifts, gratuities, entertainment, or favors from vendors or clients valued above INR 2,000 (or $25 USD) without written approval from Finance.

## 4. Information Security & Confidentiality
- **Proprietary Data:** Source code, client data, operational playbooks, and company financial records are strictly proprietary. Unauthorized disclosure or copying is illegal and subject to summary dismissal.
- **Device Security:** Acme-issued laptops must use full-disk encryption (BitLocker/FileVault) and active corporate endpoint protection. Unapproved USB drives and torrent software are prohibited.
- **Clean Desk Policy:** Sensitive paperwork and printed credentials must never be left unattended in office spaces.

## 5. Social Media & Public Communications
- Employees commenting on public forums, LinkedIn, or Twitter in a personal capacity must clarify that opinions expressed are strictly their own.
- Only designated official spokespersons (CEO Vikram Malhotra or authorized PR team) may issue formal statements to journalists or media outlets.

## 6. Disciplinary Process
Violations of the Code of Conduct are reviewed by the Ethics Committee. Disciplinary measures range from verbal warning, written reprimand, performance freeze, suspension without pay, to immediate termination for gross misconduct.
