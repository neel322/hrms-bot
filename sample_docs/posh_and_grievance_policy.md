# Acme Technologies - POSH and Grievance Redressal Policy

## 1. Policy Statement & Commitment
Acme Technologies maintains zero tolerance towards sexual harassment, workplace bullying, or intimidation. We are dedicated to providing a secure, inclusive, and gender-neutral work environment where every employee is treated with honor and dignity, in full compliance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH).

## 2. Definition of Sexual Harassment
Sexual harassment includes any one or more of the following unwelcome acts or behaviors (whether directly or by implication):
- Physical contact and advances.
- A demand or request for sexual favors.
- Making sexually colored remarks or offensive innuendos.
- Showing pornography or sexually explicit visual media.
- Any other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.
- Creating a hostile, intimidating, or offensive work environment.

This policy extends to physical workplace facilities, company-sponsored offsites, transport, client visits, and digital communication channels (Slack, video calls, emails).

## 4. Complaint Filing and Investigation Mechanism

### 4.1 How to File a Complaint
- Any aggrieved individual can file a formal complaint in writing to `posh-icc@acmetech.com` or directly to the Presiding Officer within **3 months** from the date of the incident (or last incident in a series of events).
- The complaint must describe the events, dates, individuals involved, and any supporting documentation or witness names.

### 4.2 Conciliation
- Before initiating a formal inquiry, the ICC may, upon the explicit written request of the aggrieved individual, take steps to resolve the matter through conciliation. Monetary settlement cannot form the basis of conciliation.

### 4.3 Formal Inquiry Procedure
- The ICC conducts a thorough, confidential inquiry following the principles of natural justice. Both parties are given a fair opportunity to present evidence and examine witnesses.
- The inquiry must be completed within **90 calendar days** from receipt of the complaint.
- The ICC submits an inquiry report to Executive Leadership (CEO Vikram Malhotra) within **10 days** of inquiry completion.

## 5. Protection Against Retaliation
Acme strictly prohibits retaliation, victimization, or adverse employment consequences against any person filing a complaint or participating in an inquiry. Retaliation is treated as severe misconduct leading to immediate disciplinary action.

## 6. General Workplace Grievance Redressal
For non-harassment grievances (workplace conflicts, managerial disputes, role concerns):
1. **Level 1:** Informal resolution with Reporting Manager.
2. **Level 2:** Escalate to HR Business Partner (Kavita Rao) via HRMS Grievance Ticket.
3. **Level 3:** Grievance Committee review and final resolution within 14 business days.
